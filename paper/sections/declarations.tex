\section*{Data and code availability}
The replay runner, benchmark construction scripts, registered protocol with its amendments, analysis code, figure scripts and the AAI Lab tool are available in the project repository (\url{https://github.com/linhlinhlin/AAI}). The benchmarks are derived from the public C-Pack-IPAs corpus \citep{orvalho2024cpack}; they are released as item identifiers, verified patches and labels, so that the programs themselves are obtained from the original release.

\section*{Ethics statement}
The study uses only the public, anonymised C-Pack-IPAs corpus; no new data were collected from students or instructors, and no human participants took part. The demonstration class shown in \cref{fig:tool} consists of programs written by the authors.
